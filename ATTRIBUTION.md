LearnLLM uses the topic progression and knowledge spine of Happy-LLM as a
reference, including tokenization, language modeling, attention, Transformer
architecture, training, supervised fine-tuning, parameter-efficient tuning,
evaluation, retrieval-augmented generation, and agents. The user-provided
`Happy-LLM-v1.0.pdf` was the edition consulted while designing this course.

The material has been substantially changed and reorganized for a beginner,
Windows-first, CPU-only learning path. Explanations were rewritten; large-scale
workflows were replaced by small deterministic experiments; and the runnable
code, teaching datasets, tests, verification scripts, project exercises, and
PDF generation system were newly authored for LearnLLM. This repository is not
a verbatim copy of Happy-LLM and is not an official Datawhale publication.
